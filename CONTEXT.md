# Houser domain language

## Work planning

Work planning is the household-owned process that creates a Work item, classifies it by Property, category, and Area, changes its lifecycle status, records its audit history, completes it into service history, and links supporting Documents. Quick capture, Ask Houser, inspection review, and Document intake are entry adapters to this same process; they must not define different Work item rules.

## Work item

A Work item is a durable record of maintenance, repair, inspection, replacement, improvement, or monitoring for one Property. Its status and activity history must change together.

## Owner review

Owner review confirms whether a historical inspection finding still needs work, was already completed, should be deferred, or does not apply. Findings remain in the inbox until this decision is made.
